  ```

  Please share your experience on Google – it takes 30 seconds and helps us a lot:

  ```
  (Get the link from GBP → "Ask for reviews".)
- It's fine and natural if customers mention the material and the place, e.g. "Bought M Sand and Ramco cement for my house in Guduvanchery, delivered on time". **Don't** write reviews for customers or offer anything in return for them. That's against Google's policy.
- Reply to **every** review within 24 hours, naming the product and town naturally:
  *"Thank you, Ramesh! Glad the M Sand and Dalmia cement reached your Guduvanchery site on time. – Naveen, KVM Agencies"*
- Target: 10+ new reviews every month.
